Air Freight

An extensive network of agents around the globe provides us with presence at all the major airports in the world. Backed by our strategic alliances and partnerships, we place before our customers, time sensitive services from cargo pickup from shipper's door to receiver's door.

Sea Freight

From unitised cargo (both FCL and LCL) to vessel chartering to barge services, from general to temperature controlled to hazardous cargoes, Vancouver Trans LLC offers the full spectrum of ocean freight services. Our experience of over a quarter of a century has assisted us to enter into strategic associations with some of the major ocean carriers and this has given us cost effective access to high volume and limited capacity corridors.

Land Freight

What sets Vancouver Trans LLC apart from most of its competitors is its asset-based trucking capabilities. A fleet which comprises standard vehicles of varying capacities to special purpose carriers for oversize cargoes, enables Vancouver Trans LLC to offer its customers, customised, cost effective and reliable trucking solutions of superior quality.

Warehousing and Distribution

Vancouver Trans LLC has contracted dedicated cargo care facilities in Dubai to accommodate general as well as temperature controlled cargoes. Our service spectrum encompasses receiving the cargo, warehousing, pick and pack services, kitting and inserts, cartage, distribution and delivery services, inventory control and total document management.

Custom House Solutions

As a registered customer broker in Dubai, Vancouver Trans LLC is equipped to provide solutions for every need of the customer. Further its association with cross border customs clearance agents and its global network of agents enable Vancouver Trans LLC to offer Customs clearance solutions across the world where laws and requirement are ever-changing.

Project Forwarding

The project forwarding professionals at Vancouver Trans LLC have, behind them, a track record of moving some of the most challenging cargoes, within the Middle East region and outside. Be it a Terex CC 8800, the largest of cranes, or the entire ground granulated blast furnace slag supply for a sea port project or the relocation of offshore oil field equipment, the project team at Vancouver Trans LLC has done it all. They are adept in all aspects of a project move such as routing and mapping, document management, liaison with statutory bodies, carrier selection, cargo management, costing and accounting.
